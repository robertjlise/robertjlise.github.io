The Project

Built From Scratch to Show I Could

This was a self-initiated project. I created two fictional companies — Safe Haven Homes and Strongate Locks — along with realistic competitive scenarios, to demonstrate the full process behind building effective sales battlecards.

The goal wasn't to fill space in a portfolio. It was to prove I understood the discipline well enough to construct a realistic, usable example independently — from competitive research through final design.

The real skill here: Knowing what information matters to a sales rep mid-conversation, and structuring it so they can find it in under 10 seconds.

The Approach

Research First, Design Second

Each card was built through a structured process: defining the competitor's positioning, identifying their genuine strengths, surfacing their real weaknesses, and then — most importantly — translating all of it into specific talking points and rebuttals a rep could actually use.

The design decisions were equally deliberate. Layout, hierarchy, and language were all evaluated against one question: can a rep find what they need in under 10 seconds?

Audience-Centered

Structured for sales reps mid-conversation. Clarity and speed prioritized above comprehensiveness.

Visual Hierarchy

Key differentiators and rebuttals surfaced at a glance. Supporting detail available but never in the way.

Strategic Positioning

Each card frames strengths in direct contrast to the competitor — not generic claims, specific talking points.

Field-Ready Format

Designed to work on screen or printed. No scrolling required to find the key points.
